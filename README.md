# Accord — Co-Parenting Communication Platform

Accord is a product concept for structured co-parenting communication, record integrity, and court-ready documentation.

The project is designed around a simple product principle: communication records should remain clear, organized, and usable by the people who may need to review them later — including parents, attorneys, and court personnel.

## Product goals

- Support structured communication between co-parents
- Preserve clear, timestamped records
- Reduce ambiguity in exported documentation
- Provide organized, court-ready record packages
- Separate consumer communication workflows from professional review workflows
- Keep the core experience accessible to end users while supporting a professional portal model

## Product concepts

### Neutrality firewall
A product-layer concept designed to keep the system focused on factual communication and record integrity rather than taking sides in disputes.

### Exhibit builder
A workflow for organizing selected records into structured exports intended to simplify documentation review and preparation.

### Professional portal
A proposed paid portal for attorneys and court personnel that supports review and document workflows while allowing the core parent-facing product to remain free.
